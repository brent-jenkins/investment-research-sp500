The stopping condition agreed was: if enabling the gate materially moves a
19-year backtest, it is firing on ordinary data gaps and the change stops.

A literal backtest diff CANNOT be run, and that is a property of the design
rather than an omission. The gate is live-only because the backtest engine
builds its event stream from each ticker's OWN rows -- a ticker whose data
stops simply stops being a candidate there, so enabling the gate cannot
change a backtest by construction (verified directly; recorded as the
EXEMPT_LIVE_ONLY reason in tests/test_one_sided_decision_rules.py).
